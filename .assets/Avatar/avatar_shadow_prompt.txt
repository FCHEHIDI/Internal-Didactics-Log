# Avatar Shadow / Silhouette — Image Generation Prompts

## Artistic Direction
A stylized, abstract silhouette for use as an avatar or profile identity image.
Must feel scientific, intellectual, slightly anonymous. Monochrome. High contrast. Expressionist minimalism.

---

## Variant 1 — Geometric Shadow Figure
"A minimalist human silhouette constructed entirely from geometric shapes — triangles, rectangles, circles.
Black figure on white, or white on black. No facial features. Abstract yet recognizably a person. Profile crop."

## Variant 2 — Fractal Edge Portrait
"A portrait silhouette whose edges are replaced by fractal noise — the outline dissolves into recursive detail.
Monochrome. Black on white. Expressionist. Suggests intelligence and depth without revealing identity."

## Variant 3 — Diffraction Profile
"A human profile rendered as a light diffraction pattern — the shape emerges from interference fringes.
White on black. Scientific, ethereal, precise. Suitable as an avatar or identity symbol."

## Variant 4 — Grid Dissolution Figure
"A standing figure rendered only as a disruption in a fine grid — the body is where the grid bends and breaks.
Monochrome. Expressionist. The figure is implied rather than drawn. Square crop."

## Variant 5 — Topographic Head
"A head-and-shoulders silhouette filled with dense topographic contour lines, as if the skull is a terrain map.
Black contours on white ground. Scientific, cartographic, intellectual. Avatar-format crop."
