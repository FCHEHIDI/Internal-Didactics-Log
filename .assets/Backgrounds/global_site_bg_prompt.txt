# Global Site Background — Image Generation Prompts

## Artistic Direction
Geometric abstraction, fractal structures, monochrome or duotone, scientific / intellectual / exploratory mood,
expressionist minimalism, high contrast, modern research-lab aesthetic.

---

## Variant 1 — Fractal Grid Expanse
"A vast monochrome fractal grid dissolving into recursive geometric fog. High contrast, black background with fine white
lines forming Mandelbrot-adjacent lattice structures. Scientific minimalism. Ultra-wide aspect ratio. No text."

## Variant 2 — Penrose Dust Field
"Penrose tiling fragments scattered across a deep black field, each tile slightly luminous at its edges.
Duotone: black and cold white. Expressionist, mathematical, meditative. Borderless, seamless tile."

## Variant 3 — Voronoi Depth Map
"Voronoi diagram rendered as a 3D depth map, extruded cells casting sharp geometric shadows on a near-black surface.
Monochrome. Stark contrast. Laboratory aesthetic. Suitable as a full-page website background."

## Variant 4 — Neural Lattice Noise
"Fine white neural-network-like lattice on pure black. Nodes pulse faintly, edges blur at distance into fractal noise.
Minimal, cold, intellectual. High resolution. Wide-format background image."

## Variant 5 — Crystalline Fault Lines
"Abstract crystalline fault-line pattern, monochrome, white fractures on black stone. Expressionist geometry.
Slight depth illusion through line-weight variation. Research-lab aesthetic. Seamless background."
