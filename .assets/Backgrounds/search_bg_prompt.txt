# Search Page Background — Image Generation Prompts

## Artistic Direction
The search page background should suggest scanning, traversal, signal detection, and pattern recognition.
Monochrome or duotone. High contrast. Dynamic geometric tension. Scientific / computational mood.

---

## Variant 1 — Radar Sweep Trace
"A radar sweep arc traced across a black field, the arc leaving a fading white trail. Concentric rings in the background,
extremely faint. Monochrome. High contrast. No text. Evokes scanning, discovery. Wide background."

## Variant 2 — Signal Noise Burst
"Abstract oscilloscope waveform exploding outward from the left edge, dissolving into noise at the right.
Black background, white waveform. Scientific, raw, dynamic. Banner-format background image."

## Variant 3 — Search Space Tessellation
"A Voronoi tessellation with one central cell dramatically larger than its neighbors, highlighted by brighter edges.
Monochrome. Suggests proximity search, nearest-neighbor logic. Full-page background."

## Variant 4 — Recursive Depth Scan
"Concentric rectangles nested to infinite depth, white on black, converging on a central vanishing point.
Expressionist, hypnotic, computational. Minimal. Full-page background suitable for a search interface."

## Variant 5 — Particle Stream Matrix
"A stream of fine white particles flowing diagonally across a black field, organized into loosely columnar structures.
Suggests data traversal, vector search. Monochrome. High contrast. Wide-format background."
