# Glossary Background — Image Generation Prompts

## Artistic Direction
The glossary background should evoke encyclopedic precision, taxonomy, and structured knowledge.
Monochrome. High contrast. Geometric or typographic abstraction. Scientific reference mood.

---

## Variant 1 — Dendrogram Forest
"A monochrome dendrogram structure — branching taxonomy tree — rendered in fine white lines on black.
No labels. Pure structural geometry. Dense, precise, encyclopedic. Full-page background."

## Variant 2 — Binary Lattice Weave
"An interlocking binary lattice pattern, like woven logic gates, monochrome, black and white.
Expressionist, cold, computational. Slight depth through overlapping planes. Background texture."

## Variant 3 — Periodic Table Ghost
"A ghost-faded periodic table grid overlaid on a black background. Only the grid lines remain — no element symbols.
White on black. Scientific, archival, minimal. Background image for a knowledge index page."

## Variant 4 — Radial Index Burst
"A radial burst of thin white lines from a central vanishing point on a black field.
Lines are evenly spaced, suggesting infinite categorization outward. Monochrome. Minimal. Wide format."

## Variant 5 — Circuit Ruin
"An abstract printed-circuit-board pattern rendered as ruins — some traces broken, others fading.
Monochrome, black background, white traces. Expressionist decay of structured knowledge. Background texture."
