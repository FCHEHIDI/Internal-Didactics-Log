# Entry Header Background — Image Generation Prompts

## Artistic Direction
Each journal entry header should feel like the opening page of a lab notebook section — focused, precise, slightly dramatic.
Monochrome or duotone. High contrast. Geometric or fractal texture. Expressionist scientific mood.

---

## Variant 1 — Angular Wave Collapse
"A sharp geometric wave collapsing into a horizontal line at the center. Black background, white angular strokes.
Expressionist, mathematical, high contrast. Suitable as a wide banner header. No text."

## Variant 2 — Interference Pattern Stripe
"A monochrome interference pattern — two overlapping circular wave systems producing a Moiré-like stripe across
a black field. Scientific, precise, cold. Wide-format header crop."

## Variant 3 — Recursive Triangle Horizon
"Recursive Sierpinski-like triangles fading into a dark horizon. Black and white. Minimal. The triangles diminish
toward the right edge, creating directional tension. Header banner proportion."

## Variant 4 — Graph Paper Abyss
"Dense graph-paper grid dissolving into black at the edges, as if the paper is burning away. Monochrome.
Cold white on black. Intellectual, archival, slightly ominous. Header image format."

## Variant 5 — Topographic Contour Slash
"Topographic contour lines slashed diagonally across a black field. Lines are pure white, extremely thin,
high density. Expressionist scientific abstraction. Wide banner format."
