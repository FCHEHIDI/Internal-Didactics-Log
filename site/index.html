In the Lab of Moving Systems

I work at the edge where systems think, shift, and reveal their structure. Most days I’m moonwalking through architectures, unspooling hidden invariants, zooming in and out of cognitive layers to see how reasoning actually moves. I explore models the way others explore landscapes — mapping flows, testing boundaries, and shaping protocols that stay elegant under pressure. My work lives between intuition and formalism, where ideas become systems and systems become tools for understanding.
